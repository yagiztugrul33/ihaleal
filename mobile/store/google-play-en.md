## Full Description — 4000 char

**Turkey's real-estate market, in your pocket.**

ihaleal combines property auctions, AI valuation and investment analysis in one app. Not just another listing site — a live marketplace.

📈 <b>Live Auction Terminal</b>
Open bidding, sealed offers and "buy now". Sealed-bid masking keeps your bid hidden until auction close. Countdown, auto bid-up (proxy bid) and risk alerts in real time.

🤖 <b>AI Valuation</b>
Estimates true market price by location, usable area and segment. Download SPK-compliant appraisal template PDF (Turkish character support).

🗺️ <b>Map Search</b>
Browse listings across Turkey on a single map. Clustering shows thousands of listings at a glance.

📊 <b>City Indices + Monthly Reports</b>
Residential, commercial and land price indices for Turkey's 10 largest cities. Monthly trend, yearly change, district comparison.

🛡️ <b>Transparency + Security</b>
Platform-mediated bids instead of direct seller contact. KYC, MASAK (AML), sealed bid masking.

🏗️ <b>Developer + Realtor Portal</b>
Project management for professional users.
