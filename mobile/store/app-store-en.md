## Description — 4000 char

**Turkey's real-estate market, in your pocket.**

ihaleal combines property auctions, AI valuation and investment analysis into a single app. Not just another listing site — a live marketplace.

**Highlights**

📈 **Live Auction Terminal**
Open bidding, sealed offers and "buy now". Sealed-bid masking keeps your bid hidden until the auction closes. Countdown timer, automatic bid-up (proxy bid) and risk alerts run in real time.

🤖 **AI Valuation**
Estimates true market price based on location, usable area and segment. Download an SPK-compliant appraisal template PDF in one tap (Roboto TR font with full Turkish character support).

🗺️ **Map Search**
Browse listings across Turkey on a single map. Filter by city, price range, mode and status. Clustering shows thousands of listings at a glance.

📊 **City Indices and Monthly Reports**
Residential, commercial and land price indices for the 10 largest cities. Monthly trend, yearly change, district comparison. A new PDF report each month.

🛡️ **Transparency and Security**
Platform-mediated bids instead of direct seller contact. KYC verification, MASAK (anti-money-laundering) compliance, sealed bid masking. Credit-card pre-authorisation and refund guarantee.

🏗️ **Developer and Realtor Portals**
Project management, launch publishing and client tracking for professional users.

**Why ihaleal?**

✓ Turkey's only sealed-bid real-estate auction platform
✓ Direct buyer + realtor + auction broker in one place
✓ All price data sourced from real Turkish sales records
✓ Mobile + web in lockstep
✓ KVKK-compliant data policy — privacy by design

Download ihaleal now and see what you should bid in Turkey's real-estate market — backed by real data.
